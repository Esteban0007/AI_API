What is Semantic Search?

Semantic Search understands the intent and contextual meaning of a query rather than just matching literal keywords. By converting text into vector concept maps, the system identifies relationships, synonyms, and crosses languages instantly.

Feature Traditional Search (Lexical) Semantic Search (ReadyAPI)
Search Logic Matches exact characters/keywords. Understands intent and concepts.
Language Limited to document language. Multilingual: Search English, find Spanish.
Synonyms Fails unless words match exactly. Succeeds by meaning ("broken screen" = "my display is shattered").
